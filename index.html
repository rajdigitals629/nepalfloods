THE STORY

Earthquakes, landslides and a devastating flood

On August 26, 2026, a sudden flash flood swept through the Bhotekoshi–Trishuli corridor in northern Nepal, severely affecting Rasuwa and downstream communities. Water, mud and debris damaged settlements, roads, bridges and hydropower infrastructure.

Early reports considered seismic activity among possible explanations, but subsequent scientific reporting pointed to an ice-and-rock or glacial collapse and resulting landslide as the trigger. The exact sequence has continued to be investigated.

Rescue teams have been working in difficult mountain terrain, with helicopters and ground crews searching for missing people and helping survivors. Because the disaster is still developing, casualty and missing-person figures should be treated as provisional.

Information on this page is a snapshot of reporting available on August 27, 2026. Always use official Nepalese authorities for live warnings.
